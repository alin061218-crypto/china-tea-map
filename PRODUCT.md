# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors exploring Chinese tea culture through an interactive ink-wash map. They browse tea-producing regions, discover famous tea varieties by clicking map markers, and view tea details — all within a single immersive full-screen map interface.

## Product Purpose

Tea Atlas presents Chinese tea regions through an interactive SVG map with ink-wash aesthetics. Province boundaries are rendered with watercolor washes, mountain terrain is built from layered raster PNGs, and tea varieties are plotted as blooming floral markers. Success means the map feels like an animated ink-and-watercolor print that invites exploration — poetic, tactile, and restrained.

## Core Experience

The entire product is a **single-page interactive map**. Users pan and zoom across China, hover provinces to see regional atmosphere effects, click tea markers to open detail panels, and browse the tea catalog through a bottom-sheet popup. Seasonal filtering shifts the map's color palette and bloom intensity. The experience prioritizes visual atmosphere and discovery over information density.

## Brand Personality

Poetic, tactile, restrained. The interface should feel like an animated ink-and-watercolor print coming alive, not like a generic AI illustration or a dashboard skin.

## Anti-references

Avoid obvious AI-generated fantasy scenery, hard-edged SVG flowers, CSS-only decorative blobs, glass-heavy SaaS cards, generic gradient hero treatments, and map visuals that look like a technical GIS interface.

## Design Principles

1. Match the reference before inventing new style.
2. Use real raster texture for ink, paper, wash, and floral material.
3. Keep interaction lightweight so the map remains the main visual event.
4. Let motion feel organic: blooming, diffusing, and settling rather than bouncing or popping.
5. Preserve readability and reduced-motion behavior even when the visual layer is atmospheric.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Honor reduced-motion preferences with a static or crossfaded map state. Keep controls legible over the artwork and maintain keyboard/click targets for map controls and tea details.
